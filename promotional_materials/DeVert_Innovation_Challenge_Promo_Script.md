# DeVert Innovation Challenge - Cohesive Promo Video Plan (24s)

**Concept:** "The Spark of Revolution"
**Total Duration:** 24 Seconds
**Format:** 9:16 (Vertical)
**Visual Thread (Continuity):** A persistent **"Digital Cyan Spark/Glow"** and **Dark Industrial Atmosphere** connects all three clips. The lighting should always be low-key with strong teal/cyan accents.
**Audio Note:** NO background voices or diegetic speech. Only SFX and your Voiceover.

---

## 🟢 Segment 1: The Spark (0:00 - 0:08)
**Visual Connection:** The "start" of the spark. It appears as a reflection in the eyes/glasses.

*   **Gemini Veo3 Prompt:**
    > Vertical 9:16 video. Hyper-realistic cinematic footage. A close-up profile shot of a young developer in a dark room. **Visual Continuity:** Intense Cyan lighting illuminates their face from a screen. **Action:** The developer stares intensely at a screen, motionless except for heavy breathing. Do not show them speaking. Suddenly, a digital "glitch" reflection flickers in their eye, marking a realization. **Style:** Dark industrial background, anamorphic blue lens flares, film grain, Arri Alexa footage.
*   **Audio (SFX & BGM Only):**
    *   **BGM:** Deep, rising bass drone.
    *   **SFX:** Electrical hum, computer fan whir, a sharp digital "zap" sound when the reflection flickers.

---

## 🟢 Segment 2: The Fire (0:08 - 0:16)
**Visual Connection:** The spark expands. The "Digital Cyan" is now the color of the marker/diagrams and the chaotic energy in the room.

*   **Gemini Veo3 Prompt:**
    > Vertical 9:16 video. Hyper-realistic handheld camera. A dark collaborative workspace. **Visual Continuity:** The room is washed in the same Cyan glow from Segment 1. **Action:** Three developers are working furiously. One is sketching complex diagrams on a glass wall, but the marker ink glows with a faint neon cyan hue. Another types rapidly. No talking, just intense focused action. **Style:** High contrast, slight slow-motion (60fps), sweat textures on skin, floating dust motes in the blue light.
*   **Audio (SFX & BGM Only):**
    *   **BGM:** Driving industrial rhythm kicks in.
    *   **SFX:** Aggressive keyboard typing (ASMR style), marker squeaking on glass, no voices.

---

## 🟢 Segment 3: The Explosion (0:16 - 0:24)
**Visual Connection:** The spark becomes the brand. The Cyan glow solidifies into the logo.

*   **Gemini Veo3 Prompt:**
    > Vertical 9:16 video. 3D motion graphics. The same **Cyan digital energy** from the previous clips swirls violently in a dark void and condenses to form the metallic 'DeVert' logo. **Visual Continuity:** The background texture matches the dark industrial walls of the first two clips. **Action:** The logo locks into place with a heavy glitch effect. 'Innovation Challenge' text appears below. 'COMING SOON' pulses in the same Cyan color. **Style:** Photorealistic ray-tracing, premium material textures (glass and obsidian).
*   **Audio (SFX & BGM Only):**
    *   **BGM:** Massive bass drop and triumphant synth sustain.
    *   **SFX:** Sci-fi energy implosion sound, digital glitch stutters, heavy cinematic "thud" on logo reveal.
