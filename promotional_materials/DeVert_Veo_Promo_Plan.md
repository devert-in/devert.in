# DeVert.in - 24s Cinematic Trailer Plan (Gemini Veo Edition)

**Total Duration:** 24 Seconds  
**Clip Count:** 3 Clips (8 seconds each)  
**Theme:** High-Stakes Cyberpunk / Digital Revolution  
**Tone:** Movie Trailer, Outstanding, Aggressive

---

## CLIP 1: The Awakening (00:00 - 00:08)
**Goal:** Hook the viewer immediately. Establish the "Action Movie" vibe.

*   **🎙️ Voiceover Script:**
    > "The code you write today... determines who rules tomorrow. Stop drifting in the static."
*   **🎬 Gemini Veo Prompt (Visuals):**
    > Cinematic cyberpunk shot, photorealistic 8k. A hooded coder sits in a dark, rain-soaked server room, face illuminated by the harsh glow of curved monitors. The screens display cascading green matrix code that suddenly glitches violent red, then stabilizes into a bright Neon Cyan radar pulse. The camera zooms in rapidly, pushing through the screen's pixels into a digital void. Moody lighting, volumetric fog, blade runner aesthetic.
*   **🔊 Audio/SFX:**
    > Rain against glass. Digital static noise that abruptly cuts to a deep, resonant synth drone.

---

## CLIP 2: The Arsenal (00:08 - 00:16)
**Goal:** Show the "Loot Box", "Hackathons", and "Gamification" as high-tech weaponry.

*   **🎙️ Voiceover Script:**
    > "Enter the command center. Elite hackathons. Real bounties. This is your arsenal."
*   **🎬 Gemini Veo Prompt (Visuals):**
    > Futuristic holographic interface (FUI) floating in void space. High-tech widgets labeled "MISSION: HACKATHON" and "BOUNTY: $50,000" lock into place with mechanical weight. A sci-fi 'Loot Box' crate slams down into the center frame and cracks open, revealing glowing trophies and energy icons. Fast, dynamic camera movements tracking the UI elements. Iron Man HUD style, sleek, neon green and black palette.
*   **🔊 Audio/SFX:**
    > Heavy mechanical "Lock and Load" sounds (like a gun reloading). High-pitched digital chirps. A metallic impact when the box lands.

---

## CLIP 3: The Initiation (00:16 - 00:24)
**Goal:** The Climax and Call to Action. Brand reinforcement.

*   **🎙️ Voiceover Script:**
    > "Don't just play the game. Rewrite it. DeVert.in. Initiate your sequence."
*   **🎬 Gemini Veo Prompt (Visuals):**
    > Epic title reveal. A massive, heavy metal vault door blasts open outward, sending sparks and smoke flying. Through the opening, a blinding white light reveals the text "DeVert.in" floating in 3D space, composed of shifting laser grids. The camera flies through the text into a hyperspace warp tunnel. Subtitle text "INITIATE_SEQUENCE" flashes rapidly. Blockbuster movie trailer ending, lens flares, chromatic aberration.
*   **🔊 Audio/SFX:**
    > Explosion/Blast sound. A rising "Shepard Tone" (tension builder) that peaks at the word "DeVert". Sudden silence or a "power down" sound at the very end.
